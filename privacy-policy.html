Presub Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Presub does not collect personal information. There is no sign-up or login. Speech recognition and translation run entirely on your device, and we do not operate any server that stores your data. No advertising or analytics SDKs are included.

1. Data We Collect

The app does not collect, store, or transmit your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers. There is no account or login, and we do not identify or track users.

2. Speech Recognition and Translation — On Device

The app turns spoken words in video or audio into text and translates that text into subtitles. All of this runs on your device, using the capabilities Apple provides in iOS (on-device speech recognition in the Speech framework, on-device translation in the Translation framework).

ItemDetails
AudioNever leaves your device. It is not sent to our servers; we have no way to receive it
Text for translationPassed to the on-device translator. Not sent to our servers
MicrophoneThe app does not use the microphone. It only handles audio from files or pages you open
Language dataSpeech and translation language data is distributed by Apple and stored on your device

3. App Data

ItemDetails

What is storedGenerated subtitles, settings (languages, caption size, and so on), recently opened addresses
TransmissionNone — we do not operate a server that receives data
How to delete“Delete all saved subtitles” / “Clear recent addresses” in Settings, or delete the app

Files you open may be copied to the app’s temporary folder for processing and are removed when processing finishes or when the app restarts.

If you use iCloud Backup, app data may be included in your own Apple account backup according to Apple’s device backup policy. That is a feature Apple provides, and we cannot access it.

4. The In-App Browser

The app includes a browser screen where you can open video addresses. It uses the standard iOS web component (WKWebView).

The app offers a one-time unlock. Payment is handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect or store your card or payment details. Purchase status is verified through information Apple provides and is recorded only on your device.

6. Advertising and Analytics

The app shows no advertising and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA). It contains no third-party analytics SDK and does not track user behaviour. Aggregate, anonymous statistics in App Store Connect are collected and provided by Apple and do not identify individual users.

7. Third Parties

Because we collect no personal information, there is none to share with or process on behalf of third parties.

8. Children’s Privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not ask for age or collect personal information. Because it contains a web browser that can open arbitrary websites, its App Store age rating is set accordingly. Guardians can restrict use with device features such as iOS Screen Time.

9. Your Rights

We hold no personal information about you, so there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, or delete. Data stored on your device can be removed in the app or by deleting the app.
